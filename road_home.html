Step 4 — Lectio Divina
Sacred reading — 20 min
Open Magnificat app for today's Mass readings. Four passes: Read → Meditate → Pray → Contemplate. Write one sentence in your journal.
1. Lectio — Read slowly, out loud. What word catches you?
2. Meditatio — Read again. Why this word, today, for me?
3. Oratio — Read a third time. Respond to God in prayer.
4. Contemplatio — Read once more. Put it down. Rest in silence. Let Him work.
